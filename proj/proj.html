Grounded Distractor Probing

A Diagnostic Protocol for Hard-Distractor Sensitivity in VLM Multiple-Choice Evaluation

Computer Vision and Image Processing Lab · Sogang University

The problem

When accuracy hides how a model answers

A VLM may answer a multiple-choice visual question correctly without relying on the fine-grained image evidence that the question is designed to test. Easy-to-eliminate distractors and option-order effects can obscure this difference.

Grounded Distractor Probing (GDP) is a controlled diagnostic protocol that compares matched original, permutation-control, and hard-distractor versions of the same item. Its hard distractors are same-type, visually plausible near misses that require careful attention to the image.

GDP studies whether evidence-first prompting actually changes performance under these harder conditions. Its contribution is the diagnosis of model-dependent intervention effects—not a new prompting technique or a claim of universally better accuracy.

Method

Three matched evaluation conditions

For a given item, GDP holds the image, question, and reference answer fixed while varying the multiple-choice options in a controlled way.

01

Original

Use the original multiple-choice item as the baseline.

02

Permutation control

Change option positions without altering their semantic content, to isolate ordering effects.

03

Hard distractor

Replace one incorrect option with a same-type, visually plausible near miss that demands precise image evidence.

What we observe

A controlled intervention is not a universal fix

Model-dependent responses

Evidence-first prompting can improve some LLaVA-style models, while Qwen2.5-VL and InternVL2.5 exhibit negative effects under the reported diagnostic setup.

Why the controls matter

Permutation-control and answer-first comparisons help separate hard-distractor sensitivity, option-order behavior, and different prompting-intervention mechanisms.

GDP-Slice: careful diagnosis

98 manually reviewed diagnostic items drawn from VMCBench development data, spanning TextVQA, InfoVQA, DocVQA, ChartQA, and RealWorldQA task families.

GDP-v2-lite: a scale check

An auxiliary, automatically proposed and filtered collection checks whether the main patterns remain visible at a larger scale. It is not a replacement for GDP-Slice.

Takeaway. Hard-distractor performance and intervention effects should be reported alongside aggregate accuracy. A prompting intervention that helps one model may harm another.
